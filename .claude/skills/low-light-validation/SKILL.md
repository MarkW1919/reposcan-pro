# Low-Light Validation Skill

## Purpose
Validate whether a change actually improves night performance under realistic capture conditions.

## Inputs
- before and after results
- representative night or no-light scenes
- hardware and exposure assumptions

## Workflow
1. Compare results across glare, bloom, blur, low contrast, and mixed illumination cases.
2. Separate capture-quality failures from model failures.
3. Evaluate whether changes improved usable reads, not just confidence scores.
4. Record failure modes that still require imaging or data fixes.

## Output
- validation summary
- failure-mode analysis
- recommendation to keep, revise, or revert the change
